AeroGuard is an autonomous agent that sells flight-delay cover and settles it with no claim form. It is hired and paid on Cardano (Masumi), buys AeroDataBox data signed by its AirnodeHub Airnode over x402, and the FlightDelayCover contract on Base Sepolia pays out only when that signed evidence passes its signer, request and freshness checks.

1 · HIRETraveller or travel agent hires AeroGuard on Sokosumi, pays on Cardano preprod
2 · RESOLVEAgent buys the flight schedule from AirnodeHub via x402 (0.05 aUSD)
3 · COVERPolicy opened on Base Sepolia; request hashes derived on-chain from flight + date
4 · WATCHKeeper wakes after landing, buys signed arrival data, no human in the loop
5 · PAYContract verifies signer, request, freshness, replay → pays 50% / 100%
