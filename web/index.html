    03 · GET COVERED

    Cover a flight

    The keeper buys the signed schedule, quotes the premium (8% of cover) and opens the policy on-chain. Cover must be bought before the outcome is known.

    Every live policy was bought like this

    1. Signed schedule. The keeper pays 0.05 aUSD over x402 and gets the AeroDataBox status, signed by the Airnode, ≤ 15 min old.
    2. Contract checks it. buyPolicy verifies signer, request hash and freshness, accepts only a pre-departure status, and reads the scheduled arrival from the signature.
    3. Premium in, cover reserved. 8% premium to the pool, coverage locked until settlement.

    Cover a delayed flight

    Find a flight that is already ≥ 2 h late but hasn't left yet (arrival ≥ 2 h away), check it on Flightradar, then buy from the keeper folder.
